v1.1.1-stable Apache-2.0 github.com/echocc00/SecOpent ↗

Overview

SecOpent is a catalog-driven, agent-native pentest workbench for authorized engagements. The LLM proposes; a deterministic layer + humans decide. Cases are written in YAML, executed inside a seccomp sandbox, and validated by an oracle that does N/N re-runs to catch flaky tests.

17 adapters across asset/web/network/cloud/identity mean you can chain a real-world campaign without hand-coding glue. Three layers of evidence with redaction give you a clean report at the end.
