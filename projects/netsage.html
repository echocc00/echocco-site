Overview

NetSage is an AI-driven platform that takes over the network engineer workload: design, config generation, troubleshooting, deployment, and audit. Eight specialized agents collaborate under a three-gate pipeline — every change passes Containerlab simulation, Batfish validation, and human approval with snapshot rollback before it touches a real device.

It speaks multi-vendor (Huawei VRP, Cisco IOS-XE, H3C, Juniper, Arista) and reads source-of-truth from NetBox or Nautobot. The 30-rule baseline (CIS + vendor hardening) catches the dangerous stuff automatically.

Pain → Solution → Impact

Pain

Network ops depend on deep vendor-specific knowledge — and a single typo can take down a region.

Solution

10 agents + three-gate engine, multi-vendor from day one.

Impact

Deploys in hours, not days. Outages become rare and recoverable.
