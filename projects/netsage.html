## overview

NetSage takes over the network engineer workload: design, config generation, troubleshooting, deployment, and audit. Eight specialized agents collaborate under a three-gate pipeline - every change passes Containerlab simulation, Batfish validation, and human approval with snapshot rollback before it touches a real device.

Product impact

Pain → Solution → Value

Network operations depend on deep vendor-specific knowledge that few engineers actually hold — and a single misconfig can take down a region. NetSage replaces tribal CCIE work with a coordinated team of 10 specialized AI agents, a three-gate engine, and multi-vendor support out of the box.
